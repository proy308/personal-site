The Strategic Pivot Lab

1:1 mentoring for high-performing lawyers and specialists who want to move beyond the "Support Function" label—into Deal Strategy, Chief of Staff, VC, or international roles.

The Problem

"You have the intellectual rigor for the C-suite, but the market views you as a 'Cost Center.' You don't need to learn business—you need to translate your expertise into Commercial Authority."

Who This Is For

Most people who come to the Lab fall into one of these situations.

The Law Firm Associate

You're at a Tier 1 or Tier 2 firm—corporate, M&A, capital markets, or securities—and want to move into Deal Strategy, a PE/VC fund, or an in-house role with real commercial ownership. You need a narrative, not just a resume update.

The Stuck In-House Lawyer

You're 8–15 years in, good at your job, and can see the ceiling clearly. The path is either GC or more of the same. You want a lateral move into Chief of Staff, strategy, or a revenue-adjacent function—but don't know how to position for it.

The International Pivot

You want to move to the UK, Middle East, Singapore, or Europe. Your Indian credentials are strong, but you're unsure how to position them, which qualifications matter (SQE? LLM?), and how to break into international firms without starting over.

The Policy & Strategy Specialist

You have a regulatory or public sector background and want to move into tech policy, government affairs, or commercial strategy at a large company. You need to reframe "regulatory insight" as business-critical—not just advisory.

The Philosophy: Bias for Action

The "Safe" Path

Most high-performers suffer from Analysis Paralysis. You treat career moves like "One-Way Doors" (irreversible risks), leading to 5-year plans that never happen.

The Lab Approach

You can't think your way into a new career; you have to act your way into it. We identify low-risk lateral moves and "runway experiments" you can run before you quit your job.

What You Walk Away With

Each session is 1:1, structured around your specific situation—not a generic playbook.

  • A reframed career narrative. Not "I'm a lawyer looking to move." A sharp articulation of the business problem you solve and why your background is the moat—not the liability.
  • A concrete target role shortlist. Deal Strategy. Chief of Staff. Legal Operations. Founder's Office. VC. We identify which roles are realistic in 6–18 months given where you are now—and which are traps.
  • Stakeholder translation. Learning to speak the language of the P&L owner, the hiring manager, or the investor—not the language of your practice area.
  • Network introductions where relevant. Direct warm intros to people in the roles or firms you're targeting—when it makes sense.

Why I Run This

I'm a transactional lawyer who made the pivot. After years in law firms and in-house legal roles, I moved into a deal strategy function at AWS—sitting with the sales team, owning deal closure on accounts north of $200M, and working directly with customers. That transition wasn't planned. It was built by doing the work, earning trust, and then being in the right position when the door opened.

I started the Pivot Lab because the lawyers I mentor kept saying the same thing: "There's no one to talk to about this who's actually done it." I've done it. This is that conversation.
